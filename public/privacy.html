Privacy Policy — Al Quran

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Al Quran is designed to work without creating an account. This policy explains what information the app stores and when it communicates with third-party services.

Information stored on your device

Bookmarks, notes, highlights, reading progress, favorites and app settings are stored locally on your device using browser/app storage. Al Quran does not operate a user account service for these items in the current release.

Network requests

Some features require network access. The app may request Quranic translations, tafsir or related content from Quran Foundation, and may load recitation/media resources from their respective content providers. These services receive ordinary technical request information such as IP address, request time and network headers as part of delivering content.

Quran Foundation

Where Quran Foundation APIs are used, requests are made to provide Quranic content requested by the user. Al Quran does not use Quran Foundation content or user information to build advertising profiles.

Analytics and advertising

The current release does not intentionally include advertising, advertising identifiers or analytics tracking.

Sharing and exported media

When you create a Quran image or video, generation is performed by app features using the selected content and media. Files you choose to save or share are then handled by your device and the destination you select.

Data retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you remove it, reset the relevant app data, clear site/app storage or uninstall the app. Content obtained from Quran Foundation is not intended to be stored beyond the limits permitted by Quran Foundation's developer terms.

Children

Al Quran does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account or profile system because the current release does not provide such a system.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app adds accounts, analytics, advertising, cloud sync or other data-processing features. The updated policy will be published at this location.

Contact

Until a dedicated support address is published, privacy or support enquiries should be sent through the developer contact method shown on the official Al Quran app-store listing.
